Our Story

From a single oven and a worn recipe book to a bakery that's part of the neighbourhood's daily ritual.

Where It Began

A Kitchen, a Dream,
and a Recipe

Bon Bakery was born in 2012 in a small kitchen in Gurugram. Our founder, Ananya Mehta, had grown up watching her grandmother bake sourdough every Saturday morning — a ritual she carried in her heart long after moving to the city.

What started as weekend bakes shared with neighbors quickly became something bigger. Within a year, Ananya had left her corporate job and opened the doors to the first proper Bon Bakery.

Today, over a decade later, we still use those same handwritten recipes — with a few learned refinements along the way.

Our Philosophy

No Shortcuts. Ever.

We believe in the slow way. Our sourdough ferments for at least 72 hours. Our croissants are laminated by hand with real French butter. Our cakes use single-origin chocolate and seasonal fruit.

We don't use stabilizers, artificial flavors, or preservatives. Every item is baked in small batches by trained bakers who show up before dawn because they genuinely love what they do.
